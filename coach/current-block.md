## Current priorities

2. **Fat loss** — 0.5 kg/week target, deficit 15–18%, preserve lean mass

## Phase definitions

```
Base (current) — Aerobic foundation, consistency, fat loss
  Focus: volume building, habit establishment, body composition
  Weeks 1–4 (~22 Jun – 19 Jul)

Build — Longer runs, higher volume, gym integration, race-specific work
  Focus: long run progression toward 14+ km, tempo introduction, sustained volume
  Weeks 5–6 (~21 Jul – 3 Aug)

Deload — Recovery week
  Focus: volume reduction, recovery, assess readiness for peak
  Week 7 (~4 Aug – 10 Aug)

Peak — Maximum training load, longest runs
  Focus: highest volume weeks, long runs 11–14 km, final hard training
  Weeks 8–9 (~11 Aug – 24 Aug)

Taper — Volume reduction, freshness, race prep
  Focus: reduced volume (40–60% of peak), maintain intensity, nutrition shift to maintenance
  Week 10 (~25 Aug – 31 Aug)

Race Week — Final preparation
  Focus: light movement only, carb loading Thu/Fri, race logistics, mental prep
  Week 11 (1 Sep – 7 Sep, race 6 Sep)
```

Phase transitions are coaching decisions. They are documented in the coaching log with evidence for why the transition is happening.
